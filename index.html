About RAUN

The friend who throws the best parties

RAUN Event & Decor is a South Florida event styling and rental company based in Tamarac, led by owner Rinaude Merard. We handle the details behind the scenes — design, décor, rentals, setup, and teardown — so you can be joyful out front with your guests.

From intimate birthdays to 200-guest celebrations, every event gets a custom look and a custom quote. No two events are the same — and neither are our designs.

  • Custom décor design for your theme & colors
  • Full rental inventory: tables to glassware
  • Private event space — 185 seated / 200 standing
  • Photo, video & DJ available on request

FAQ

Questions, answered

How far in advance should I book?

There's no strict cutoff, but popular dates — especially weekends in season — go fast. Reach out as soon as you have a date in mind and we'll lock it in.

How much does event decor cost?

Every event is unique, so we work by custom quote — it depends on your guest count, decor, rentals, setup, and delivery. Tell us what you're planning and we'll build your personal quote.

Is a deposit required?

Yes — a deposit secures your date. The exact amount is shared with you clearly before you book.

What is your cancellation policy?

Life happens — we get it. Our cancellation and rescheduling policy is shared with you clearly before you book, so there are no surprises.

Do you deliver, set up, and tear down?

Yes! We deliver, set up, and tear down — depending on the package you choose. You enjoy your event; we handle everything behind the scenes.

What areas do you serve?

We're based in Tamarac, Florida and serve all of South Florida — Miami, Fort Lauderdale, Hollywood, Pembroke Pines, Coral Springs, Boca Raton, West Palm Beach and beyond. We travel anywhere from 10 minutes to 2 hours away depending on your event.

Can I rent tables and chairs without full styling?

Absolutely — you can rent tables, chairs, linens, chargers, glassware, silverware and more without the full styling service.

Will you match my theme and colors?

Yes! Every design is custom — we match your theme, colors, and style from the first sketch to the final table.

Do you offer photography, video, or DJ services?

We offer decor packages, and photography, video, and DJ services can be added on request. One team, one vision, zero stress.

How many guests does your event space hold?

Our private event space fits 185 guests seated and 200 standing — a blank canvas we style your way.
